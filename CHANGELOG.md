## [0.1.0] - 2026-07-25

### Added

- Initial release: a CPU-rendered starfield screensaver for egui,
  recreating the classic Windows 95/98 "Starfield Simulation" screen
  saver — stars radiating outward from the center of the screen with
  warp-speed streak trails, as if flying through space.
- Adjustable `speed` and `density` fields.
- A web (WASM) demo example.
